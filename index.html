Profile

I use images and words to notice what makes a place feel singular.

Hi, I’m Joshua. My creative path started with photography, a quiet hobby that gradually became a way of paying attention.

Working alongside Softer Volumes turned that habit into a practice. It’s where I built the toolkit I still use today—from research and design to the small, considered details that hold a body of work together.

When I’m not working, I’m usually planning the next trip or wandering somewhere new with a camera.
